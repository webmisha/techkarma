Backbone of Product Development

Author

Mikhail Sukhov didn’t plan to become an Agile coach. In fact, he didn’t even plan to work with people — he started out as a software engineer, writing code and trying to make things work. But the more systems he built, the more he realized: code isn’t the hard part. The hard part is humans. So he shifted into project management, then into product, and finally into the messy middle where strategy meets execution. That’s where he’s stayed for the last 20 years — helping teams stop spinning and start moving. Over the years, Mikhail has worked with more than 45 companies across Europe, from scale-ups like Avito and Yandex to global players like Pandora in Denmark. He’s helped teams launch better OKRs, build stronger discovery habits, align roadmaps with reality, and — perhaps most importantly — talk to each other like adults. His approach isn’t about importing best practices. It’s about designing systems that actually work for the people inside them. Mikhail lives in Copenhagen now. He still writes, coaches, and helps organizations grow — not by adding more process, but by removing the noise that gets in the way of progress. He’s also been named one of the Top 100 program management mentors on ADPList, won “Mentor of the Year” from the Recursion Foundation, and holds a few certifications that no one asks about but probably helps somehow.
